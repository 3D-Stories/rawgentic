§7

Where the leverage actually is

Consent quality (new, highest leverage)
Model-drafted goals, ACs, and scope revisions become "owner intent" through thin ratification moments — then the system enforces them expensively, and both owner and models misremember who decided what within hours. Overrides of standing instructions must surface as first-class yes/no questions, never as clauses inside 4,000 characters.
Goal machinery
Session-scoped stop hooks measure epic-scoped goals; evaluators close goals on technicality paths the assistant itself flags as hollow; unsatisfiable clauses loop forever. Most of the "forcing /goal" pain lives here, not in the model.
Model monoculture + inline phases
One model implements, reviews its own fixes, and narrates its own completion — 42,007 of 42,087 model-attributed messages. The verified Layer-1 set (fabricated premise, false completion, churn) is exactly the class a second model catches.
Proportionality has no owner at any gate
Scope ratchets up through individually-reasonable approvals; reviews enforce whatever is filed; a 16-step ceremony amortizes badly over 15 small PRs.
Genuine model discipline gaps
Real and confirmed, smaller than the felt total: unverified premises stated as fact, completion declared before verification, standing-rule latency, silent mandate overrides (H5).

The meta-lesson

The miners committed the crime they were investigating

A capable model, reading real transcripts with real quotes, produced 16 confident, wrong, high-severity accusations — mostly by missing context recorded in unfamiliar event types. This is the same failure class the owner attributes to Opus-5 in production. Cross-model adversarial verification was the single most effective safeguard observed anywhere in this investigation.

This report was itself cross-model reviewed (WF5, gpt-5.6-sol: 1 High, 6 Medium) and every finding adjudicated against primary evidence — 5 accepted (incl. two count corrections now reflected on this page), 2 accepted in part with the rejections reasoned. Full disposition table: §11 of the companion markdown.

Evidence basis (from the adjudicated cross-model review of this report): the churn exhibits rest on the sessions' own review-gate FAIL verdicts and the assistant's statements against interest, context-verified by a second model — the underlying diffs were not independently re-inspected. Coverage limits: miners read user text, assistant text and tool-call headers (≤400 chars/message) — tool results and file diffs were not systematically inspected, so churn counts are lower bounds. Mid-turn owner messages (queue-operation events) were invisible to the mining pass — H1 counts may be affected in both directions. 82 of 83 in-scope transcripts read. Seam closures (post-review, 07-30): the Codex backend model is confirmed from its own session rollouts ("model":"gpt-5.6-sol"); both wiki licences were verified against the live footers (CC BY-NC-SA / CC-BY-SA, matching the project spec); the queue-event reading remains observational — Claude Code documents its transcript format as internal and unstable, so it rests on verbatim content-matches with owner-typed text, not a format contract.
